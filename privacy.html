Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

TradeMetric (www.trademetric.app) is a fantasy football tool for trades, rankings and lineups. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we keep only what the site needs to work, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, the username you pick, and your password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Google or Discord sign-in. If you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from that service. We don't get your password or access to anything else in that account.
  • Your leagues. League settings, rosters, teams and similar data you import or enter, so they follow you to any device.
  • Questions you send the AI Helper, including any screenshots you upload, so we can answer them.

What stays on your device

Some things are saved only in your own browser and never sent to our servers: your theme choice, a local copy of your league, and, if you choose to save them, your ESPN private-league codes. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save and sync your leagues.
  • To send account emails, such as confirmation codes and password resets. We don't send marketing email.
  • To answer AI Helper questions.

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use third-party ad trackers.

Services we use

A few companies handle data on our behalf, only to run the site:

  • Supabase: accounts and saved leagues.
  • Vercel: hosting the website.
  • Resend: sending account emails.
  • Anthropic: powering the AI Helper. Questions and screenshots you send it are passed to Anthropic to generate an answer.
  • Google and Discord: only if you choose to sign in with them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and leagues until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time from Account → Security → Delete account, which permanently removes your sign-in, username and saved leagues.

Children

TradeMetric isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
